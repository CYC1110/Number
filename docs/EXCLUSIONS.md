# Exclusions and unresolved items

- Original checkpoints, datasets, image files, result numbers, source archives, manuscript text/PDF/DOCX and hand-edited SVG assets are not copied.
- Private project instructions/settings, progress notes, field reports, internal research proposals, notebooks/outputs, shell histories, logs and hardware/user metadata are not included.
- Legacy aggregate registries, damaged-checkpoint recovery, external-drive fallback, hidden virtual environments, symlinks and checkpoint initialization from historical hashes are removed.
- Unneeded PCA/RSA/brain-similarity, dimensionality pilots, 105k synthetic pilots and unrelated exploratory branches are excluded. Original files are preserved in place.
- Older cohort variants and geometry P6/adaptive-EV outputs are not blended with this condition-specific n=20/n=5 P7 N1/P2 reference.
- Final manuscript identity is unconfirmed. `260925B` is the evidenced coverage candidate, not a certified final publication.
- Author names/affiliations/email addresses are withheld. License and third-party asset redistribution permissions remain undecided.
- Official WMM images, shader programs/templates, ImageNet, stored DVD images and frozen FID features are user-supplied inputs; none is fetched or included.
- Human behavioral data/insets and original SVG composition are unavailable as reproducible numerical inputs. New layouts do not reproduce those assets.
- The compact reference training/readout/pair/context recipes have disclosed differences from historical recipes. This package is for method review; no retrospective performance or numerical agreement is claimed.
- Existing private outputs have not been certified for publication. The privacy check applies to the new source package only.
